Key equations
1D motion
v = v₀ + at
Δx = v₀t + ½at²

2D and vectors
vₓ = v cosθ
vᵧ = v sinθ
v = √(vₓ² + vᵧ²)
θ = tan⁻¹(vᵧ/vₓ)

θ measured from the horizontal: cos gives the horizontal part, sin gives the vertical part.
